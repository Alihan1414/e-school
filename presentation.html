Bank-Grade Privacy & Data Protection

Enterprise Security & Identity Protection

Schools manage sensitive student data. E-School Daara provides robust security mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access.

🔑

2-Factor OTP Recovery

Forgot password requests generate a time-limited 6-digit cryptographic OTP transmitted to the registered contact for identity verification.

🔒

Self-Service Password Manager

Every logged-in user (Admin, Teacher, Student, Parent) can securely update their credentials at any time directly from their profile.

📜

Immutable Audit Trail

All sensitive operations (account creation, deletion, grade changes, contract dispatches) are logged with IP and timestamp in `audit_logs`.
